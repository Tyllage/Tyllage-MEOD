# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Tyllage is a proprietary project maintained by the Tyllage team; it does not accept outside contributions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). We, the Tyllage team, pledge to make working on this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. This applies regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful and constructive in reviews, issues and meetings
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the team, MEOD Farm and its customers
- **Protecting the privacy of MEOD staff, customers and group-buy participants**

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information (including MEOD customer data) without explicit permission
- Sharing confidential MEOD business information outside the project
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code applies to Tyllage team members in all project spaces: GitHub, team chats, meetings, and any time you represent the project, for example when working with MEOD. It also applies to anyone who interacts with those spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers, or to your course supervisor at NYP. Every report will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict anyone whose behaviour breaks this Code.
