# Problem Statement 6B — Improving Sales and Marketing Outreach Through AI

**Industry sector:** Agriculture / AgriTech / eCommerce
**Company:** MEOD Farm

| Dimension | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| **Who** | MEOD's sales and marketing team, its customers, and group-buy leaders |
| **What** | MEOD Farm struggles to scale its sales and marketing work. Stock and order enquiries are handled by hand, promotions are run ad hoc, records live in Excel, and group-buy settlement is slow |
| **Barriers** | Systems that don't talk to each other, repetitive WhatsApp conversations, no reliable shared view of customers and orders, and privacy-sensitive participant data |
| **Cause** | **Limited digital-marketing manpower** and a lack of automation. There is no full-time marketing staff, so campaigns are ad hoc |
| **Emotion** | Frustrated by low visibility and overwhelmed by manual work |
| **Outcome of problem** | **Weak lead conversion** and missed opportunities |
| **Value proposition hoped from AI** | Automated campaign generation, personalised offers and lead insights. *Can AI auto-segment customers and test messaging?* |
| **Requires company data?** | Yes. This includes sales logs, WhatsApp groups and past promotions |
| **Systems the PoC would work with** | MEOD Fresh (Shopify) store, WhatsApp Business, Excel/group-buy records, and a possible Microsoft system (to be confirmed) |
